Booking, cancellation and membership terms

Last updated: 22 November 2026

These terms govern bookings for classes, private sessions, passes and subscriptions at Mystika by Mer in Balance. Bookings are made through the Lorari platform. By booking, you accept these terms.

  • Owner: María Socias Moreno (self-employed individual)

  • Email: info@mystikabymerinbalance.com
  • Trading name: Mystika by Mer in Balance

2. Bookings

Places are reserved on the Lorari platform, using the credit or payment applicable at the time of booking. A booking is confirmed once it appears in your profile.

3. Cancellations

  • With 12 hours or more notice, you can cancel your booking free of charge. The class is returned to your credit.
  • With less than 12 hours' notice, or if you do not attend, you lose the credit for that class.

Private sessions, astrology and tarot readings and personalised services have their own conditions, which are confirmed at the time of booking.

4. Passes and subscriptions

  • No minimum commitment. There is no minimum contract period.
  • No enrolment fee or sign-up charges.
  • Automatic monthly renewal. The subscription renews each month unless you cancel it.

5. How to cancel your subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your profile on Lorari, by indicating that you do not want to renew or that you want to stop using the service. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid; the next monthly payment will not be charged. Cancellation can be made through the same channel used to subscribe, without needing to call or write to anyone. If you have any difficulty cancelling, write to us at info@mystikabymerinbalance.com.

6. Right of withdrawal

For passes and subscriptions you have the right to withdraw within 14 days of purchase, provided you have not used the classes included. Classes and sessions for a specific date cannot be withdrawn from once they have been provided, in accordance with consumer law.

7. Prices

Prices published on the website are indicative and include VAT. Current prices are those shown at the time of booking on Lorari.

8. Complaints

If you wish to make a complaint, you can write to info@mystikabymerinbalance.com or use the official complaint forms, which can be requested at the studio. You can also submit your complaint through the Generalitat de Catalunya consumer form (consum.gencat.cat).

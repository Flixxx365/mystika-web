Here, no class is just a class.

Some sessions focus on training; others bring in astrology, tarot, meditation or journaling. They all share one purpose: to move, reconnect and find your balance.

Essential movement

The foundation: strength, control and mobility.

BarreStrength, grace and movement.

A dynamic class that combines elements of ballet, Pilates and functional training to build strength, muscular endurance, balance and coordination.

Through controlled movements, small repetitions and flowing sequences at the barre and on the mat, you will work your whole body, especially legs, glutes and core.

A challenging, fun practice where precision and consistency take centre stage. No ballet experience needed: just the wish to move and enjoy yourself.

Ideal for: toning, building muscular endurance, improving balance and breaking out of your routine.

Small movements. Big changes.

Pilates MatConnect with your body. Strengthen from within.

A mindful practice that combines strength, control, stability and mobility to help you build a stronger, more balanced relationship with your body.

Through exercises on the mat, you will work your deep muscles, core, posture and coordination, paying attention to your breath and to the quality of every movement.

A class to gain strength and stability, improve your posture and feel more connected to yourself, at your own pace.

Ideal for: improving strength, posture, body control and mobility.

Your body is your home. Learn to live in it.

YogaFind the balance between movement and stillness.

A practice that connects body, breath and mind through postures, mindful transitions and moments of full awareness.

During the session we will work on mobility, balance, flexibility and strength, adapting the practice to the pace and needs of the group. The breath will help us stay present in every movement and create a space to disconnect from the outside world.

Depending on the style of the session, the practice may be more dynamic and flowing or slower and more restorative.

Ideal for: improving mobility, cultivating presence and finding a balance between activity and rest.

Breathe in presence. Breathe out what you no longer need.

Mystika experiences

Movement with intention. Barre or Pilates format, depending on the schedule.

Astro BarreMove with intention. Connect with your energy.

Astro Barre brings together the intensity and precision of barre with the inspiration of astrology to create an experience that goes beyond conventional training.

We will start by activating the body with a barre sequence designed to build strength, endurance and control. Each class follows an astrological theme that guides us in connecting movement with the moment we are living.

Depending on the session, we will explore a sign, a planetary energy, a moon phase or a theme of self-discovery, through short reflections and ideas to carry that intention into your everyday life.

Ideal for: those who want to train with energy and add a layer of self-discovery to their practice.

Movement can also be a way of listening to yourself.

Mystic FlowOne class. One card. A message for you.

Mystic Flow is an experience that combines mindful movement, inner connection and the symbolism of tarot.

Through a movement practice, we will create a space to release the tensions of the day, come back to the body and listen to how we feel. At the end, you will intuitively choose a card and discover the message that comes with it.

Each card comes with a personal reflection and a journal prompt, so you leave with more than the feeling of having trained: a small space to observe yourself and connect within.

Format: Barre or Pilates, depending on the scheduled class. The format is shown in the booking schedule. Ideal for: those looking to combine movement, introspection and a different kind of experience.

It is not about finding every answer, but about asking yourself the right questions.

Lunar FlowHonour your cycles. Move at your own pace.

A mindful movement practice inspired by the cycles of the Moon and by the connection with our inner world.

Through movement sequences, we will explore a way of training that is more connected to our sensations and needs. Each session may be inspired by a moon phase or by a theme related to cycles, intuition, new beginnings or the process of letting go.

The experience closes with an intention or reflection that will stay with you beyond the mat.

Format: Barre or Pilates, depending on the scheduled class. The format is shown in the booking schedule. Ideal for: those who want a mindful practice with room for inner connection and self-discovery.

You don’t always have to be at your brightest. You also need space to turn inward.

Slow MoonSlow down. Breathe. Come back to yourself.

A space to slow down and give yourself a moment of calm in the middle of your routine.

Slow Moon combines gentle movement, stretching, mobility, conscious breathing and relaxation to release tension and build a kinder connection with your body.

The practice unfolds without rushing, respecting how each person feels and leaving room for pause and presence. It may include moments of meditation, short reflections or journaling prompts inspired by the theme of the session.

Format: Pilates or Barre, depending on the scheduled class, at a slow and mindful pace. Ideal for: those looking to switch off, improve their mobility and find a space of calm and wellbeing.

Not all growth needs to be rushed.

Solar HeatActivate your strength. Ignite your energy.

A dynamic experience inspired by the energy of the Sun, vitality and self-confidence.

Through a more energetic movement practice, you will work on strength, muscular endurance and body control. The intensity of the session lets you challenge yourself, break out of inertia and connect with your energy through movement.

Solar Heat stands for action, determination and the ability to take up your own space. An experience to finish the class feeling strong, active and present.

Format: Barre or Pilates, depending on the scheduled class, always with a dynamic, energetic focus. Ideal for: those who enjoy dynamic workouts and want to build strength, endurance and energy.

Take up your space. Trust your strength. Shine your way.

About Mer

This is not just a studio.
It is my story.

For a long time I did what I was supposed to do. Until a moment came when I no longer recognised myself in that life.

Coming back to my body, listening to myself and building a life more aligned with who I am changed the way I understand wellbeing.

Mystika was born from that process, and from the wish to create a space where other women can also pause, connect and find their own rhythm.

Frequently asked questions

Before your first class.

Do I need any previous experience?

No. Every class adapts to your level, and you will always have options to make each exercise gentler or more intense. If it is your first time, Barre or Pilates Mat are a great place to start.

Is Mystika a Pilates studio?

It is a movement studio: barre, Pilates and yoga. Some classes add astrology, tarot, meditation or journaling, but movement is always at the heart of it.

What makes the classes different?

Each experience has its own intention. In some the focus is training; in others, the practice is paired with a moon phase, an astrological theme or a tarot card with a journal prompt.

How do I know if a class is Barre or Pilates?

Mystic Flow, Lunar Flow, Slow Moon and Solar Heat can be in Barre or Pilates format. The format of each session is shown in the booking schedule.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, a bottle of water and a towel. We take care of the rest.
